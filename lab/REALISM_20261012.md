## What changed

The previous checker tested two uniform choices of quad diagonals. Blender
can rotate corners independently on different faces, so a pair of faces may
use different diagonals. A historically accepted CPU right-hand pose had
three actual Blender thumb folds, approximately 0.094, 0.536 and 1.434 mm long.
Matching production vertices back to the CPU reconstruction confirms the
same crossings; renderer precision is not their cause.

The corrected final audit tests both uniform sets and mixed choices on
different faces. Only incompatible alternatives of the same face are
excluded from the mixed check. Each within-face triangulation still checks
genuine folded/bow-tie quads. Geometric shared-feature exemptions remain;
sharing a vertex is not a blanket collision exemption. All three buckets
must be clear. Counts describe possible triangle pairs, not physical events.

Optional optimization witnesses now include small interior patches for
unexpected incident-face folds and mixed choices. These are conditioning
tools; full, untrimmed surface checks remain mandatory. They do not establish
positive mesh distance, containment, or continuous-time safety. The default
optimization mode stays `legacy`; the stronger final audit always applies.
