# Rendered skin feasibility experiment — 2026-10-11

Hypothesis: enforcing actual rendered skin clearance and supported skin/key
contacts in anatomical wrist/joint coordinates improves feasibility compared
with a fixed wrist and sampled tube penalties. A single pose is insufficient
evidence for a playable, collision-free performance.

The NumPy normalized LBS and Catmull-Clark limit-surface evaluator agrees with
Blender within 0.005441 mm on both hands at frames 24, 50 and 68 of the saved
overlap probe. Vertex mapping is bijective and all rendered quad faces match.
All keys and case solids match within 0.000096 mm. One source vertex's accidental
opposite-hand weight was repaired identically in the renderer and CPU evaluator.

The shared piano solids include physical key gaps, tilted key bodies, keybed,
front rail, cheeks, fallboard, lid, music desk and floor. The static audit checks
complete level-2 rendered skin vertices and both diagonals of every quad, using
triangle/box SAT at 0.01 mm interior tolerance. This catches triangle crossings
whose vertices are all outside a solid.

## Follow-up: contact patch, triangle constraints and motion

The L-frame-24 failure had a concrete anchor error. The mesh vertex nearest an
approximate internal pad was 1.77 mm above the actual supporting underside.
Selecting the lowest distal skin patch near that pad, retaining that patch's
existing tangential placement, clears the pose in 3.85 s. It retains note 45,
leaves no vertex/triangle instrument penetration, and moves the wrist 0.002 mm.
An unsuccessful solver candidate now returns the unchanged input hand; a
regression test verifies that even an optimizer's fake success cannot export an
out-of-bounds candidate.

A connected window at frames 23–25 was solved with unchanged anatomy, original
timestamps, fixed shoulders, actual note anchors, neighboring joint/wrist speed
bounds and added wrist-orientation/elbow-plane bounds of 4 rad/s (engineering
limits). Clipped triangle-point witnesses accept 4/6 hand poses. Enforcing
whole-triangle separating-axis margins accepts 6/6. Both methods audit both
diagonals of each rendered quad. The production Blender surface/topology
comparison on all three corrected frames passes, with maximum error 0.001171 mm.

**Counterevidence:** the six clear exported poses do not form a clear path.
Anatomical joint/root interpolation, SO(3) wrist interpolation, exact two-bone
elbows and interpolated key depths collide on 18/21 observations at 240 Hz.
Maximum depth is 5.5905 mm in the left hand and 1.3354 mm in the right. All three
exported instants remain clear. This empirical audit does not prove continuous
time clearance even if all its samples eventually pass.

The worst left-hand interval has a premature key return. MIDI/feeling timing
places note 45 at 247.9323–248.8637 s. At 248.0417 s the source pad slips off the
front, and key 45 returns even though the score/assigned hold continues. Requiring
assigned sustained score contacts after the existing 80 ms settling interval
exposes an additional infeasible solve, rather than falsely treating the key
command as physical support. That failed candidate is rejected. This experiment
needs coupled key/contact/wrist replanning; commanding held key depth alone is
insufficient.

Visual inspection of the left-hand front views at frames 23/24/25 finds free
fingers too straight and the posture visibly strained. Numerical clearance is
not a form or naturalness pass. These are hand-scale-1 stress probes; the
production calibrated scale 0.728 also requires separate generated validation.

## Sustained-contact follow-up: bounded initialization and finite exposed tops

The constrained descent from an intersecting pose was unstable. An optional
bounded trust-region feasibility initialization now reduces constraint
violations before the original hard-gated SLSQP optimization. Numerical angle
slacks use 50 mm/rad conditioning; this leaves every physical speed bound's
zero crossing unchanged. A feasible optimized pose is retained when it reduces
positive slack, while a worse infeasible or nonfinite step cannot poison the
initialization. Both changes have regression coverage. This is an opt-in
diagnostic controller, not a promotion of the complete production performance.

The first frozen-neighbor initialized attempt was rejected: 18 triangle
intersections remained and the wrist angular bound failed. That failure is
retained. After correcting conditioning/candidate retention, a controlled pair
on the identical frozen left-frame-25 input/source/score hashes accepts the
initialized pose and rejects the original start. The original candidate has
5.714 mm penetration; the accepted candidate has zero instrument intersections,
11.038 rad/s maximum joint speed and 2.037 rad/s wrist angular speed. The wrist
moves about 4.8 mm. Only the initialization budget differs within that pair.
This is one pose, not the 258-input ablation or a trajectory proof.

An independent held-note check exposed false contact acceptance in the earlier
six-pose held-contact window. Its contact points could slide about 1 mm off a
key's front while remaining inside their 2 mm placement tolerance. Vertical
gap constraints alone did not establish physical support. That window's
claimed contact acceptance is superseded; its zero instrument intersections
at exported instants remain valid. It misses 43 of 74 required held-note
observations during the sampled window.

The pose solver now enforces the actual finite footprint and the unilateral
top gap, and independently verifies the anchor on the exposed moving top.
Black-key occlusion is checked against the rotated solids. A point outside the
key or below its underside cannot count as support. All six new poses pass
instrument, finite-contact, anatomy, proxy spacing and temporal bounds. All
14 requested anchors pass; the independent held-score check has no unsupported
required pitches at the three exported instants. Blender surface/topology
parity passes at all three frames within 0.001238 mm.

**The connected motion still fails.** At 240 Hz, 18/21 observations collide,
maximum 3.3281 mm, and 10/74 required held-note observations lack support.
Eighteen sampled hand observations have negative proxy spacing, worst
-0.00973 mm. Observed joint/wrist/angular/elbow speeds stay within the
engineering bounds. This separates bounded motion from collision-free motion.
The two six-pose windows also differ in conditioning/candidate retention, so
their comparison cannot isolate the effect of footprint constraints alone.
Independent actual-skin support uses a 0.1 mm top gap with 0.01 mm geometric
tolerance, key depth at least 0.5, and an 80 ms score settling interval. Onset,
accidental-note completeness and forces are excluded by this check.

Visual review of the new right-hand frames 23/24/25 shows an overcurled posture
and contacts too close to the front edge. The earlier left-hand view has overly
straight free fingers. Neither view passes human naturalness. Next experiments
need human-calibrated contact placement and connected wrist/finger/key paths,
plus complete rendered self/interhand/body/cloth geometry and CCD.
